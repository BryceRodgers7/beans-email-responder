The Mental Gain is a sports mental performance company. I work with teams, parents, coaches, and individual athletes on mental skills training. I teach concepts such as confidence, bouncing back from mistakes, performing under pressure, motivation, ideal mindset, and much more. For individual athletes, this is accomplished through 30 or 50-minute sessions where an athlete meets with me via Zoom (or in-person, if requested) and we do activities, reflections, discussions, watch videos, and more. In general, I recommend weekly sessions, and once we see significant improvement, we gradually taper off. Most athletes show positive improvement around 6 sessions and more significant improvement around 10 — however, this is VERY dependent on commitment, severity, age, awareness, motivation, etc. Just a rough timeline for you.

*Mental Performance Session Options*

At The Mental Gain, we want to make sure mental performance is accessible to all athletes that want it, and can work with any schedule. Therefore, we have 2 mental performance session options to choose from.

*Option A - Online Mental Modules + 30-minute 1-on-1 Implementation Sessions -* This option includes hand-picked mental skills lessons (created by me) that are completed online by the athlete on a flexible schedule. These modules/lessons have videos, activities/games, reflections, and more to help them learn. This is also paired with 30-minute weekly 1-on-1 implementation sessions with me to apply and personalize the skills they learn.

*Option B - 50-minute 1-on-1 Sessions -* This option is a traditional 1-on-1 mental performance format where the athlete meets with me for 50 minutes. The exact same mental skills, videos, and reflection questions from the online modules are taught here. However, they are instead taught by me during our sessions.
